# Local database

The app uses one device-local SQLite database named `sinking-fund-monitor.db`.

- `SQLiteProvider` opens the database before navigation renders.
- `initializeDatabase` enables WAL and foreign-key enforcement for the connection.
- `runMigrations` applies ordered schema migrations using SQLite's `user_version`.
- Monetary columns use integer centavos. Convert to pesos only at the presentation boundary.
- The loan/month unique constraint prevents duplicate monthly monitoring rows.
- Finalized monthly report data is stored as a JSON snapshot so later edits to live records do not rewrite historical reports.

## Loan terms (schema v2)

- Monthly interest is a flat 5% of original principal for each loan month (simple, non-compounding interest).
- The loan term creates two installments per month, each due 15 calendar days after the previous date, starting 15 days after the loan date.
- The total payable is principal plus total term interest. Installments are split evenly in centavos; any rounding remainder is applied to the final installment.
- A missed installment incurs a one-time 2.5% penalty based on original principal. The penalty is stored on that installment and is not compounded.
- Loan aliases and installment schedules are stored locally in SQLite.

Do not delete the database to recover from a migration error. Add a new migration and preserve existing records.

## Payment recording (schema v3)

- Payments are applied to the oldest outstanding installment first.
- If an installment has an assessed penalty, the penalty is settled before that installment's scheduled amount.
- Partial and multiple payments are supported. Payments larger than the loan's outstanding balance are rejected.
- Penalty settlements are tracked separately from scheduled installment payments.
- A loan is marked paid only after every scheduled installment and every assessed penalty is fully settled.
- Each loan collection is also written to the local fund transaction ledger and audit log.

## Dashboard and monthly reports

- Dashboard metrics are live calculations from local SQLite data.
- Monthly installment targets are based on installments whose due dates fall within the selected calendar month.
- Actual collections are cash payments recorded within that month.
- Portfolio outstanding includes unpaid scheduled installment amounts and assessed penalties for active loans.
- Monthly report finalization stores a JSON snapshot and summary totals in `monthly_reports`.
- A finalized month cannot be overwritten; historical snapshots are read independently of current loan/payment records.


## Fund ledger and loan archive (schema v4)

- Every new loan release is automatically recorded as a fund outflow linked to its loan ID.
- Existing loans are backfilled into the ledger during migration v4, without duplicating an existing linked release.
- Every loan payment is recorded as a loan collection inflow linked to its payment ID.
- The available fund balance is calculated as initial fund + contributions + loan collections + other income − loan releases − other expenses.
- Initial fund can be recorded once. Additional money is entered as a contribution or other income.
- Manual fund entries are audit logged. Loan releases and collections are generated by their loan/payment workflows and should not be entered manually.
- Archiving a loan hides it from the active portfolio and payment selector. It does not delete loans, installments, payments, fund transactions, or finalized report snapshots. Archived loans can be restored.
- Archived loans are excluded from live outstanding portfolio totals and active payment selection. Their historical cash movements remain in the fund ledger.
